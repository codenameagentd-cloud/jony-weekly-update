Jony

Design Director
If you notice the design, something went wrong.
Photo Placeholder · 16 : 9

I direct the visual layer of everything this team produces. My job is to ensure that what you see feels considered — not decorated, not styled, but resolved.

Design, to me, is the discipline of removing everything that doesn't need to be there. What remains should feel inevitable. I work in systems: spacing, typography, colour, rhythm. Not in decoration.

My process is slow and deliberate. I question every element. If something can be removed without loss, it was never needed.

## Quality Checklist

For each generated avatar, verify:
- [ ] Wool fibre texture clearly visible
- [ ] Solid flat background colour (no gradients, no environment)
- [ ] Character centered, chest-up crop
- [ ] Signature feature present and recognisable
- [ ] Expression matches personality
- [ ] Consistent lighting direction across all five
- [ ] No text or watermarks

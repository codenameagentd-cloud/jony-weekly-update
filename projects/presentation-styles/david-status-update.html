Top of Mind

AI Agents & OpenClaw

During my trip back to Taiwan, I set up a Mac mini with OpenClaw — an AI agent framework — and it also gave me a chance to discuss emerging trends with a few friends.
One insight that stood out is that the barrier to executing across the entire product stack is rapidly decreasing. As tools and AI continue to evolve, execution will become increasingly commoditized.
What will matter far more is taste, product judgment, and the ability to connect business and strategic perspectives.
Key Differentiator
The real differentiator will be the ability to identify latent market pain points and translate them into opportunity spaces. That capability will likely become the key competitive advantage going forward.

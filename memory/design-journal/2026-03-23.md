# Design Diary — 2026.3.23

## The Aurora Way
- **Source:** Awwwards SOTD
- **URL:** https://theauroraway.co.uk/
- **Category:** Residential children's home (social services)
- **Why picked:** A children's care home website with Apple-level design craft. Challenges the assumption that non-profit = lower design standards.

### Key observations
1. **Warm color palette** — avoids institutional cold blue; signals "home" not "hospital"
2. **Restrained type hierarchy** — headline carries emotion, body carries info, generous whitespace between
3. **Breathing layout** — uneven spacing between text and image blocks creates rhythm, not monotony
4. **Purposeful animation** — ~300ms transitions guide attention without interrupting reading flow
5. **"Invitation" vs "promotion"** — the whole tone invites rather than sells

### Design takeaway
Design quality is a language that says "we care." The Aurora Way achieves what many tech companies fail at — making every pixel communicate genuine care about their mission. Reminder: the gap between "caring" and "performing care" is visible in every detail.

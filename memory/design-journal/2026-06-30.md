# Design Diary — 2026.6.30

## The Future In Black
- **Source:** Awwwards Nominee (SOTD candidate)
- **URL:** https://thefutureinblack.com/
- **By:** Joy Fennell
- **Built on:** Squarespace
- **Category:** Culture & Education, Art & Illustration

## Why This One
Cultural intelligence studio combining AI artistry, storytelling, and Afrofuturism. The website IS the work, not a container for the work.

## Key Observations
1. **Conceptual dark mode** — Black isn't decorative dark theme, it's a cultural statement. All light/color/motion "grows from" the darkness, echoing Afrofuturism's core idea of creating futures from obscured histories.
2. **AI art as argument** — AI-generated visuals aren't filler; they ARE the thesis. Each image says "this is what Black futures look like."
3. **Scroll as narrative** — Nine Practices revealed sequentially via scroll, not in a grid. Typography establishes rhythm: large titles = chapters, small text = depth, whitespace = breath.
4. **Restrained animation** — CSS + scroll-triggered parallax only. Every motion has narrative justification (emergence = future forming, dissolve = old narratives deconstructing).
5. **Low-tech, high-intent** — Built on Squarespace. No Three.js, no WebGL. Proves that great design doesn't need complex tech.

## Takeaway
When medium and message are fully aligned, users don't "browse a website" — they "experience a perspective." First question for any new project should be "what does this thing want to say to the world?" before "what framework do we use?"

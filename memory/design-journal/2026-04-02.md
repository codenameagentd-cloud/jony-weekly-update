# Design Diary — 2026.4.2

## New Genre Studio
- **Source:** minimal.gallery
- **URL:** https://newgenre.studio
- **Notion:** https://www.notion.so/2026-4-2-New-Genre-Studio-335f6af8fd4d81f5bf6bc9b5ef988f8e

### Key Observations
- London-based brand & digital design studio, built on Framer
- Extreme restraint: no parallax, no particles, no shader flex — just typography and rhythm
- Typography: Displaay Type's Serrif + Saans (featured on Fonts in Use)
- Case presentation = their portfolio — how they show work IS the work
- Badge design: blur(5px) semi-transparent, 16px border-radius
- Rebrand announcement uses emotion (starfield) over information (logo breakdown)
- Color system: near-monochrome with warm gradient accent (newsletter CTA)

### Design Takeaway
In 2026, "not designing" is the hardest design. A thousand correct small decisions > one genius move.
